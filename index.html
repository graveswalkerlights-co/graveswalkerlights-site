Questions

Good to know

Can they stay outside all season?

The frame is galvanized steel, so it won’t rot the way grapevine does. Plug them in with an outdoor-rated extension cord.

How long is the cord?

Each ball is wrapped in one 21-foot strand with 100 lights. You’ll need an extension cord to reach an outlet.

How do I hang them?

Most people loop a hook or a bit of wire over a sturdy branch and run an outdoor extension cord to the plug. Hang a few at different heights for the best look.

How soon will I get them?

Every order is ready for pickup or delivered within 10 days.

Where do you deliver?

Most of Raleigh and the towns around it. Enter your ZIP code in the order form and it’ll tell you right away. Delivery is $13.99 per order, no matter how many you buy. Pickup in Raleigh is always free.

What’s your return policy?

You can return your lights within 1 year of purchase. If a ball is defective, we’ll swap it for a new one within 3 months of delivery. Call or text us to start a return or swap.
